# Autonomous Hypothesis Generation

## Overview
This skill implements RD-Agent's research ideation core, autonomously proposing theoretically grounded modeling hypotheses, feature transformations, and mathematical alpha factors tailored to target scientific or financial domains.

## Key Capabilities
- **Domain Modeling Reasoning**: Analyzes domain dynamics (financial markets, tabular distributions, RL dynamics) to formulate testable hypotheses.
- **Factor Formula Design**: Authors vectorized mathematical equations combining cross-sectional and time-series operators.
- **Literature & Feedback Grounding**: Synthesizes insights from past experimental outcomes and academic literature to avoid repetitive trials.

## Operational Workflow
1. **Context Analysis**: Ingest dataset metadata, baseline metrics, and current research objectives.
2. **Prior Knowledge Retrieval**: Query the research knowledge base for previously tested hypotheses.
3. **Hypothesis Formulation**: Draft hypothesis statement, underlying intuition, and measurable target criteria.
4. **Experiment Specification**: Define concrete input features, transformations, and baseline comparison models.
