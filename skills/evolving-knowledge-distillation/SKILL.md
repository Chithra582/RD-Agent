# Evolving Knowledge Distillation

## Overview
This skill closes the autonomous R&D loop by analyzing experimental results, extracting empirical principles, and recording structured knowledge nodes that guide subsequent research iterations.

## Key Capabilities
- **Performance Attribution**: Disentangles the contributions of specific feature transforms or architecture tweaks to final metrics.
- **Error Diagnosis**: Analyzes failed experiments to identify fundamental theoretical flaws versus trivial implementation bugs.
- **Knowledge Base Evolution**: Commits verified hypotheses and post-mortems into an evolving research graph to prevent repetitive exploration.

## Operational Workflow
1. **Metric Comparison**: Compare experiment results against baseline benchmark scores and significance thresholds.
2. **Outcome Attribution**: Determine why the hypothesis succeeded or failed based on intermediate logs.
3. **Knowledge Distillation**: Formulate concise learning summaries and update factor/model registries.
4. **Next-Step Formulation**: Generate recommendations for the next cycle of hypothesis proposal.
